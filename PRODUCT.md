# AiRE — product truth

## What it is
An emissions-compliance control room for vehicle manufacturers. It answers one
question per market: **are we over the line, what does it cost, and what do we do
about it** — for the EU, India, UK, Australia and China.

## Who uses it
- **Compliance / regulatory affairs.** Lives in it. Needs the statutory detail
  right and traceable to the source. Will be asked to defend every figure.
- **Finance / FP&A.** Needs the exposure in money, by year, with the assumptions
  visible and separable from actuals.
- **Product & planning.** Needs to model a mix change and see the fine move.
- **Executives.** Need one number and one sentence, quarterly.

## The one thing that must never break
**Every figure is computed by a deterministic engine from official-source data,
and can be traced back to it.** Not estimated, not invented by a model. The AI
explains and navigates; it never produces a number. This is the product.

## Competitive position
Watershed, Persefoni, Sphera and Sweep all sell "audit-ready" and "defensible"
carbon accounting. They are broad (all scopes, all frameworks) and shallow on any
one regulation. AiRE is the opposite: narrow to light-duty vehicle CO2 compliance,
and deep enough to be the system of record for it — real registrations, the actual
statutory target line, pooling, and the penalty.

So the product's claim is **depth and defensibility in one regime**, and the UI
has to make that legible in the first five seconds.

## Constraints that shape the UI
- Markets diverge. The EU pools and issues no credit; China has no pooled average
  and runs a dual-credit ledger; the UK is a unit mandate, not a CO2 line. A
  market's screens are NOT the same screens with different numbers.
- The data is large and real (the EU alone: 158 manufacturers, ~12M registrations).
- The output is a legal and financial position someone has to defend in a meeting.
